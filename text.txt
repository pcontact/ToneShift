* In order to make our game,shufflux, appealing, reaplayable and successful, we consider a mechanics where the
  world reshuffles (the platform/obstacles and flag position - which player must pick to earn points- changes).

  Our logic of implementing our mechanics, so far, is to reshuffle and change the obstacles and flag position whenever a 
  player picks up a flag, this applies to both solo(Time attack) and multiplayer game modes(Capture the Flag). But we think this logic has some downside,
  and the only downside we have observed so far is that it makes the game to run at the pace of the player, because the 
  world only reshuffles when a player has picked a flag.
  
  To counter this downside we are considering some simple yet effective logic, so far we are considering two logics which 
  are:
  1. to reshuffle the world at certain interval irrespective of the players action
  2. To keep it more tight, as player move the world get unstable, as they continue moving it gets more unstable and when a
     threshold is reached the world reshuffles. Now player are forced to reach the flag while covering short distance. the 
     longer the distnace they travel to the flag the world might reshuffle forcing them to now move toward another flag
     position.
